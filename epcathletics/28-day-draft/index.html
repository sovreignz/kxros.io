How the program works

Choose the level that meets you today.

You are never locked into a track. Open any day, then choose Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced for that workout. The exercises stay familiar while the volume and intensity meet your current capacity.

01

Beginner

New to strength training or returning after time off. Prioritize technique and quality reps.

02

Intermediate

Comfortable with the movements and training consistently for three to six months.

03

Advanced

Experienced, confident with form and able to train safely close to failure.

Your roadmap

The 28-day program

Coach's note

“Challenge yourself through progressive overload, but never at the expense of your form.”

Start each week slightly stronger than the last. Your final working set should feel challenging while your technique stays clean.

Keep these consistent

Your weekly anchors

01

Warm up

Begin every training session with 10–15 minutes of easy cardio, then complete the mobility sequence.

02

Cardio

Complete 30 minutes three days each week at a low-to-moderate intensity, aiming for 120–140 BPM.

03

Movement

Aim for a weekly average of 8,000–10,000 steps per day. The average matters more than perfection.

04

Progress

When form is solid, increase weight gradually from set to set and from week to week.
