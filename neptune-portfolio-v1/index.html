Our
Process

01

Enquiry & Initial Chat

Get in touch with your wedding date, venue, and a rough idea of your vision. We'll check availability and arrange a first conversation.

02

Free Consultation

We meet (in person or via video call) to talk through colours, styles, budget, and the specific pieces you need — bouquets, ceremony flowers, centrepieces, and more.

03

Consultation Summary

We put together a consultation summary which includes a inspiration visual, and a detailed quote covering every element you've asked for, so you know exactly what's included and what it costs.

04

Revisions

You review the proposal and let us know if anything needs tweaking, flower choices, colour balance, quantities — until it feels right.

05

Booking & Deposit

Once you're happy, we confirm the booking with a signed agreement and deposit, securing your date.

06

Final Details

8-4 weeks before the wedding, we finalise numbers, timings, and any last-minute changes (guest count, venue access, delivery windows).

07

Sourcing & Preparation

We order and arrange all flowers and materials in the days leading up to the wedding, ensuring everything is fresh and ready.

08

Delivery & Set-Up

Everything is delivered and installed either the day before or on the day itself, with bouquets and buttonholes hand-delivered to the wedding party. Collection is also available on request.

FAQ’S

How far in advance should we book our wedding flowers?

This really depends on the size and scale of your wedding flowers, larger weddings or peak season dates (spring and summer) benefit from booking further ahead, while smaller or more flexible weddings can sometimes be arranged closer to the date. As a general guide, we'd recommend getting in touch as soon as you start planning your big day to secure your date.

Do you offer consultations, and are they free?

Yes, we offer a complimentary in-person consultation to talk through your vision, style and requirements.

Can we bring inspiration photos or a Pinterest board?

Absolutely, we love it when couples share mood boards, Pinterest links or photos. It helps us understand your style and put together a proposal that feels truly yours.

What's your delivery area, and do you charge extra for travel?

We're based in Glasgow's West End and deliver weddings across Scotland, including the east coast and Edinburgh. Additional travel charges beyond greater Glasgow, will apply. Please reach out with your venue location to receive an accurate quote.

Do you set up the flowers at the venue, or do we need to arrange that?

We handle full delivery and set-up on the day, so you don't need to worry about a thing.

Do you offer hire items (vases, arches, plinths), and what happens if something gets damaged?

Yes, vase, arch, and plinth hire is available as part of our service. All hire items remain our property and should be treated with care throughout your event. In the case of damage or loss, the client is responsible for covering the replacement cost of the item.

Can you work with our budget, and do you have package options?

To suit different budgets we can tailor a bespoke quote based on what matters most to you.

What if my chosen flowers aren't in season?

If a specific flower isn't in season, we'll suggest a beautiful alternative that captures the same look and feel, so your vision stays intact.

Can you match specific colours or themes (e.g. a particular dress or venue)?

Definitely, we love working from colour swatches, dress fabric or venue details to make sure everything ties together seamlessly.

Do you provide flowers for both the ceremony and reception, or do we need separate arrangements?

We can cover every part of your day, ceremony, reception, and everything in between, as part of one cohesive package.

What happens to the flowers after the wedding, can we keep them?

Of course, the flowers are yours to keep.

Do you offer sustainable or locally-sourced options?

Yes. Locally grown flowers are available within their growing season, and environmentally conscious arrangement options are available on request, just get in touch to discuss.

How much of a deposit do you require, and when is the balance due?

We ask for a £150 holding deposit to reserve your date. This is only required after your initial consultation, so there's no commitment needed before then.

What's your cancellation/refund policy?

To secure your date, we ask for a £150 holding deposit following your initial consultation. This deposit is non-refundable.

If you cancel more than 30 days before your wedding, no further payment is required beyond the deposit. If you cancel between 14 and 30 days before your wedding, 50% of the remaining balance is still due.

Can you accommodate last-minute changes (e.g. guest count, extra buttonholes)?

We understand plans can change, we'll always do our best to accommodate adjustments, though we'd ask for as much notice as possible.

Do you handle allergies or specific flower requests (e.g. avoiding certain flowers)?

Yes, just let us know of any allergies or flowers you'd like to avoid, and we'll design around them.

Will you visit the venue beforehand?

In most cases this isn't necessary, as we're able to plan effectively without a site visit. However, for special requests a venue visit can be arranged, subject to an additional charge.
