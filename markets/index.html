02 / My role

One direction.
Every creative effort.

Lead a cohesive creative system across your internal team, agencies and individual creators. Research, cast, brief and learn with a shared direction.

Borrow proven formats without borrowing identity.

01Creative research & formats

Audit existing copy-trading content. Find proven formats in trading and adjacent categories, then adapt them around Markets’ positioning.

The output: a growing library of original, repeatable briefs that creators can execute consistently.

02More tests from every shoot

Test verbal, visual and text hooks alongside creator type, message, product moment, editing and pacing.

Capture 3–4 alternate openings per shoot where possible. Use trial or non-grid placements to test variations without making every version a permanent creator post.

The goal: more useful tests from the same creator budget.

03Strategic talent casting

Make creator selection part of the strategy. Source curated talent across adjacent niches, with particular attention to the 25–35 audience and other higher-value demographics.

Give internal teams and agencies clear creator archetypes built around audience, region and a creative hypothesis.

Introductions only. No commission or markup. Markets handles contracting, payment, management and renegotiation directly.

04Positioning through content

Markets already has its positioning. The job is to express it consistently across hundreds of assets: a repeated phrase, a product truth, a recurring reason Markets is different.

Make the app a recurring part of the content. Keep every brief aligned on narrative, performance and compliance, while leaving room for the creator’s voice.

05Compounding creative intelligence

Track what worked, what failed, who converted and what to test next. Repeat stronger formats, cut weaker ideas and let every cycle improve the next one.

Competitors can hire the same creators and agencies. Markets’ edge is the accumulated intelligence that determines what gets made, tested and scaled.
